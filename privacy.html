Privacy Policy for ReactCam

Last updated: September 2026

ReactCam ("the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles data.

Camera Access

ReactCam requests access to your device's camera solely to provide its core functionality. All camera data is processed locally on your device.

Data Collection

ReactCam does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, photos, videos, or usage analytics. No data leaves your device. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Accounts

ReactCam does not require or support user account creation.

Third-Party Services

ReactCam does not integrate with any third-party services that collect data.

Children's Privacy

Because ReactCam does not collect any data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted at this URL.
